# HD pack page (2026-11-12)

Game client > **HD pack …** installs fueryin's "Conquest of Azeroth – Ultra-HD Mod Pack"
(<https://github.com/fueryin/Conquest-of-Azeroth-Ultra-HD-Graphics-Modpack/tree/CoA-HD-PATCH>) into a CoA Custom game.
The player downloads the pack from Google Drive and unpacks it; AFK Realm finds it in Downloads or the player picks the
folder. Code: `src/ClientHd.cs`, `app/Web/Api.Hd.cs`, `app/wwwroot/js/hd.js`.

## Archive names

The pack names its archives for the official CoA client. In CoA Custom three of them collide with CoA Custom's own
archives (`patch-ZZE`, `patch-ZZU`, `patch-ZZZ`; CoA Custom's `patch-ZZU` holds `ChrRaces.dbc` and the character
creation), so copying the pack over the game breaks the custom races.

Listfile comparison of every pack archive with every archive of the CoA Custom 1.9.9 client (2026-11-12):

| Pack | Overlaps (game archives) | Name in the game | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| patch-ZZB, ZZD, ZZE (HD world) | patch.MPQ, 2, 3, A, WA, WC1-3, Z, CoA's creature/item patches | `patch-ZXB/ZXD/ZXE-HD.MPQ` | must beat CoA's own HD archives |
| patch-ZZU (upright orc) | patch.MPQ, 2, 3, Q, CHA | `patch-ZXU-HD.MPQ` | must beat CoA's HD character archive |
| patch-ZZA (shaders, fonts) | patch.MPQ, 2, 3, A | `patch-ZXA.MPQ` | |
| patch-ZZY (cursor) | (no listfile) | `patch-ZXY.MPQ` | |
| patch-ZZZ (blood) | M, O | `patch-ZXZ.MPQ` | |
| patch-AAP (spell effects) | DBCs of CoA Custom's ZZS, ZZW, ZZZ, ZZZZS, ZZZZX | `patch-AAP.MPQ` | must stay low: its DBCs must not replace CoA Custom's |
| patch-AAS (sounds) | (encrypted listfile) | `patch-AAS.MPQ` | low, as the pack ships it |

`patch-*.MPQ` sort descending (`PatchFiles`), the last name opens with the highest priority. `ZX` sorts above CoA's own
archives (patch-Z, ZE*, ZG ... ZVU) and below CoA Custom's (`patch-ZZE` ... `patch-ZZZZZZZZ`), which none of the pack's
files touch. A `-HD.MPQ` name is refused by CoA64 with `-hd 0` (`RealmData::IsHdOnlyArchive`), so HD models never pair with SD
skins.

## Left out

The pack's `CoA64.exe` (CoA64 0.1.1 for the official client: no CoA Custom races), `x64\d3d9.dll` (its own DXVK),
`XvidDecoder.dll`, `WTF\Config.wtf` (would replace the player's settings), ReShade and its preset.

## Switches

Each part is switched off by renaming (`.off` behind the archive or add-on folder), so a weak laptop can run without
the HD world or LuxVelum and switch them back on without the pack. LuxVelum is switched with `Enable=` in
`LuxVelum.ini` (restart needed); its in-game window is Ctrl+F7. LuxVelum needs a CoA64.exe with the LuxVelum host
(CoA64 `docs/2026-11-12-luxvelum-host.md`); the Voices page installs that exe once the voices release carries it.

## Dance music

The pack's DanceMusic picks its song at load time from `UnitRace` and has songs for the ten classic races only, so
CoA Custom races stay silent. The page writes `assets/hd-pack/DanceMusic.lua` over it: the song is chosen when you
dance, other races get their donor race's song, `/dancemusic test` plays it and says when game music is off.
